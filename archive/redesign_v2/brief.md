# UX/UI Analysis & Redesign Brief

## Analysis of Current State
The current site utilizes a standard dashboard layout (Sidebar + Content). While functional, it lacks the "wow" factor and immediate clarity required for a high-stakes betting model. The information density is likely high (tables), which is poor for mobile users who want quick answers: "What do I bet on?"

## Redesign Goal
**Appealing & Intuitive: "The Premium Betting Terminal"**
The new design will focus on:
1.  **Mobile-First**: Bottom navigation bar, thumb-friendly touch targets, swipeable cards.
2.  **Visual Hierarchy**: The "Best Bet" should be the biggest thing on the screen.
3.  **Aesthetic**: Dark mode with "Cyberpunk/Fintech" vibes. Deep blacks, glowing neon accents for EV+ bets (Green), Warning colors for high risk (Orange/Red). Glassmorphism for depth.

## Key Screens
1.  **The "Action" Dashboard (Home)**
    - **Header**: User's Bankroll & Today's PnL (Profit and Loss).
    - **Hero Section**: "Top Play of the Day". A large, detailed card with the highest confidence bet.
    - **Quick List**: "Next Up" - Chronological list of upcoming high-value bets.
    
2.  **Predictions / Market**
    - Filterable list of all plays.
    - Categorized buckets: "Safe Plays", "Lotto Tickets", "Player Props".
    - Usage of *Sentiment Chips*: "High Confidence", "Sharp Money", "Model Convergence".

3.  **Tracker / History**
    - Visual graph of performance over time.
    - Calendar view of past wins/losses.

## User Experience Improvements
- **"Tell me what to do"**: Instead of just showing data, the UI will use imperative language: "BET THIS", "STAY AWAY".
- **Confidence Visualization**: Don't just show "56%". Show a gauge or a progress bar that is visually striking.
- **One-Tap Context**: Tapping a card should expand it to show *why* the model likes it (logic, stats), but initially, keep it simple.
